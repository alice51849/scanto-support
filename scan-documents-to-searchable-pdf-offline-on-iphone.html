Why “searchable” and “offline” matter more than image quality

Most phone scans look fine. The problems show up three months later: you need the receipt for a warranty claim, the contract clause about notice periods, or the ID copy for a rental application, and the file is called Scan 2026-07-14 09.42.pdf. Searching inside the documents, not just by file name, is what turns a pile of scans into an archive.

Offline matters for a different reason. IDs, contracts, pay slips and medical paperwork are the documents people scan most often, and they are exactly the ones you would rather not hand to a server. A scanner that never goes online removes that question entirely.

Scanning a multi-page document with ScanTo Pro

  1. Scan. Fast multi-page scanning with automatic edge detection and perspective correction. Reorder, rotate, delete and add pages anytime.

Isn’t the iPhone’s built-in scanner enough?

For an occasional one-page form, often yes: the Notes and Files apps can scan documents too. A dedicated scanner earns its place when you scan regularly and need to find things again: text search inside every document, automatic sorting into receipts, IDs and contracts, names generated from the contents, an ID layout, encrypted exports and a separate app lock for the archive.
