What still works with zero bars

Everything. That’s the point of the app. Nothing is uploaded and there is no cloud, so there is nothing to wait for:

  • Multi-page scanning with automatic edge detection and perspective correction. Reorder, rotate, delete or add pages whenever you want.
  • Text recognition (OCR) on the device. Every scan is read automatically, so you can search for a word and find the document that contains it, even before you have signal again.
  • Auto-sorting and auto-naming. Scans land in Receipts, IDs, Cards, Contracts and more, and are named from what’s on the page, not “Scan 14.”
  • PDF export sized to US Letter, A4 or the original image, ready to print.
  • ID Card mode puts the front and back on one page (part of the one-time unlock).
  • Face ID lock on the app itself, plus password-protected, encrypted PDFs for anything sensitive (part of the one-time unlock).

Quick tip: going somewhere with no signal? Open the app once and check that it works with airplane mode on before you need it. It takes ten seconds.

Getting the PDF out once you’re back online

The PDF lives on your iPhone until you decide where it goes. Save it to the Files app right away, so it’s easy to find later, or share it to Mail, AirDrop or Messages. For a lease or an ID copy, export it as a password-protected PDF (included in the one-time unlock) and send the password some other way, by text or a quick phone call, not in the same email as the file.

And when someone asks for “that receipt from the trip” three weeks later, type a word you remember from it, like the hotel name or a total, and the search will look inside the documents themselves, not only at the file names.

FAQ

Can I scan to PDF with no Wi-Fi and no cell signal?

Yes. ScanTo Pro works exactly the same in airplane mode. Scanning, text recognition, sorting and PDF export all run on the iPhone itself, so there is nothing to wait for.

Does the text recognition (OCR) need an internet connection?

No. OCR runs on the device and reads every scan automatically. You can search for a word inside your documents, or copy the recognized text, while you are still offline.

Do I need to sign in or create an account first?

No. There is no account, no sign-up and no login, so you will not get stuck at a login screen in a place with no signal.

Where does the PDF go if I can’t send it yet?

It stays on your iPhone. You can save it to the Files app right away and share it later to Mail, AirDrop or Messages.

What can I do with the free download?

Scanning, photo import and on-device text search are unlimited for everyone. Your first 3 PDF exports are free and carry a watermark. A single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited exports without the watermark, plus OCR text export, password-encrypted PDFs and the ID-card layout. There is no subscription and no ads.

Will the PDF print on regular US paper?

Yes. You can export PDFs sized to US Letter, A4, or the size of the original image.
