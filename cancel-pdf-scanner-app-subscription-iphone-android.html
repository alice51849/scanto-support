How to cancel a PDF scanner app subscription on iPhone or Android, and ask for a refund

Short answer: deleting the app does not stop the charges. A scanner app subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play is cancelled in your Apple Account or Google Play settings, not inside the app, and refund requests go to Apple or Google. The steps below are taken from Apple's and Google's own help pages, checked on 27 November 2026. ScanTo Pro for iPhone, which we make, has no subscription, so this page is about other scanner apps.

Cancel a scanner app subscription on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the scanner app's subscription.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. You may need to scroll down to find the button.

If there is no Cancel button, or you see an expiry message in red text, the subscription has already been cancelled.

Can't find it in the list? You may have bought it with a different Apple Account. Apple suggests searching your email for “receipt from Apple” or “invoice from Apple”; the receipt shows what was bought and which account was used. If there is no receipt from Apple at all, Apple says you may have bought the subscription from another company; check your bank or card statement and contact the company that bills you.

Source: Apple Support: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Cancel a scanner app subscription on Android

  1. On your Android device, open your subscriptions in Google Play.
  2. Select the subscription you want to cancel.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the instructions.

Make sure you are signed in to the Google Account that has the subscription; if you can't find it, it may be on a different account. Google's help page puts it plainly: when you uninstall the app, your subscription won't cancel. After you cancel, you can still use the subscription for the time you have already paid for.

Source: Google Play Help: Cancel, pause, or change a subscription.

Ask for a refund

App Store purchases

  1. Sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Tap or click “I'd like to”, then choose “Request a refund”.
  3. Choose the reason, then choose Next.
  4. Choose the subscription or item, then choose Submit.

Apple says to wait 24 to 48 hours for an update on your request. If the charge is still pending, you can't request a refund yet; try again once the email receipt arrives. A refund request and a cancellation are separate steps, so if you no longer want the subscription, cancel it as well. Refund eligibility varies by country or region, and Apple notes that where consumer law protections apply, including Australia and New Zealand, you keep your rights under those laws.

Source: Apple Support: Request a refund.

Google Play purchases

  1. Go to play.google.com and click your profile picture at the top right.
  2. Click Payments & subscriptions, then Budget & order history.
  3. For the order you want refunded, click Report a problem.
  4. Select the option that describes your situation, complete the form and note that you'd like a refund, then click Submit.

Google says you'll usually get a decision within one day, but it can take up to four days. If you don't find the order, you may have bought it on a different Google Account. You can also contact the app's developer directly; Google says that is often the quickest way to resolve the issue. Refund policies differ by what you bought, when and how you paid, and where you are, and Google lists separate rules for the UK and the European Economic Area.

Sources: Google Play Help: Request a refund on Google Play and refund policies.

Whether a refund is approved is decided by Apple or Google. This page cannot promise an outcome.

If you are still in a trial period

Apple says that if you signed up for a trial and don't want to renew it, you should cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The subscription screen shows the renewal date, so check it today rather than on the last day.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting the scanner app cancel the subscription?

No. Deleting or uninstalling an app does not cancel a subscription. You have to cancel it in your Apple Account or Google Play subscriptions, using the steps on this page.

How do I cancel a scanner app subscription on iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap the scanner app's subscription, then tap Cancel Subscription. If there is no Cancel button, or you see an expiry message in red text, it has already been cancelled.

How do I cancel a scanner app subscription on Android?

Open your subscriptions in Google Play, select the subscription you want to cancel, tap Cancel subscription and follow the instructions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund for a scanner app subscription I did not want?

You can ask. For App Store purchases, sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com, choose "I'd like to", then "Request a refund", pick a reason and the item, and submit. Apple says to wait 24 to 48 hours for an update. For Google Play purchases, go to play.google.com, open Payments & subscriptions, then Budget & order history, and choose Report a problem on the order; Google says a decision usually takes one day and can take up to four. Approval is decided by Apple or Google and is not guaranteed.

When should I cancel a trial so I am not charged?

Apple says to cancel a trial at least 24 hours before it ends. Check the renewal date shown on the subscription screen and cancel before then.

Is there a document scanner with no subscription?

Yes. The Notes app on iPhone and Google Drive on Android both scan documents without a separate paid app. ScanTo Pro is another option. On iPhone, scanning is unlimited and free, the first 3 PDF exports are free with a watermark, and a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited exports with no subscription. On Android, a free version is included and an in-app purchase unlocks the full version.
