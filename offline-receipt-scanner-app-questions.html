Offline receipt scanner app: questions before you download

Short answer: yes, you can scan receipts on iPhone, have the text recognized on the device, and find any receipt later by typing a word that appears on it, without an account and without uploading anything. What ScanTo Pro does with receipts

  • Scan: multi-page scanning with automatic edge detection and perspective correction, or import a photo you already took.
  • Read: on-device text recognition (OCR) reads every scan automatically.
  • Sort: scans are auto-sorted into Receipts, IDs, Cards, Contracts and more, offline.
  • Find: search for a word and get the document that contains it, not just a file name.
  • Share: export PDFs sized to the image, A4 or US Letter, save to Files, or share to Mail, AirDrop or Messages.

A simple receipt routine that holds up

  1. Scan the receipt the same day, before thermal paper fades.
  2. Pick the Document enhance or B&W look for faint print.
  3. Let ScanTo Pro name and sort it; Smart auto-naming uses the document's contents.
  4. When you need it, search the store name or a word you remember from the receipt.
  5. Export the PDF only when someone actually asks for it, for example for a reimbursement or a return.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline, with no account?

Yes. There is no cloud and no account, and it works the same in airplane mode. Scans and recognized text never leave your device.

Is there a receipt scanner with no account or sign-up?

Yes. ScanTo works with no account, no sign-up and no login, and it runs the same in airplane mode, so your receipt scans never leave your iPhone. On-device text recognition sorts receipts into their own group automatically, and you can find one later by typing a word printed on it.

Can I find a receipt later by the store name or an item on it?

Yes. Because every scan is read by on-device text recognition, you can type a word that appears on the receipt and find the document that contains it.

Will it add up my expenses or build an expense report?

No. ScanTo Pro is a scanner and searchable archive, not an expense tracker. It recognizes the text so you can search, view and copy it, and you can export PDFs (and the recognized text as a .txt file with the one-time unlock) to use in whatever tool you already keep your expenses in.

Can I scan several receipts quickly?

Multi-page scanning detects edges and corrects perspective automatically, and you can reorder, rotate, delete and add pages at any time. You can also start a scan hands-free with Siri, Spotlight, Shortcuts or the Action Button.

Can I keep receipts private if they show card details?

Lock the app with Face ID or your passcode, and export password-protected, encrypted PDFs when you need to send a document to someone.

What is free, and what does the one-time unlock add?

Scanning, photo import and on-device text search are unlimited for everyone, and your first 3 PDF exports are included (free exports carry a watermark). A single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited PDF exports without the watermark, plus OCR text export, password-encrypted PDFs and the ID-card layout. No subscription and no ads.
